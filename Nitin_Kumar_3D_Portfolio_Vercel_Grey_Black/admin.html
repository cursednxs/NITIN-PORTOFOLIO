How to add images to your portfolio:
  1. Go to imgbb.com (free, no signup needed)
  2. Click "Start Uploading" → select your design image
  3. After upload, click "Direct links" → copy the "Direct link" URL
  4. Paste that URL in the "Image URL" field below

Or add images to your repo: put files in assets/designs/ and use paths like assets/designs/poster1.jpg
After adding items: Go to the "Export JSON" tab → download data/projects.json → replace it in your GitHub repo → push. Vercel auto-deploys!

How to Add Images — Step by Step

1

Go to imgbb.com
No signup needed. It's free forever.

2

Click "Start Uploading" and select your design image (JPG, PNG, etc.)

3

After upload, find "Direct links" dropdown → copy the "Image URL"

4

Come back here and paste the URL in the "Image URL" field

5

Fill the rest and click "+ Add"

6

Go to "Export JSON" tab, download, and replace in your GitHub repo!

Alternative: Add images directly to your repo

Create a folder assets/designs/ in your repo. Put your images there (e.g. poster1.jpg). Then use the path assets/designs/poster1.jpg as the Image URL. Commit and push along with the updated JSON.
